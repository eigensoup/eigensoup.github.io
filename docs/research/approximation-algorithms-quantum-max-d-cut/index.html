arXiv:2309.10957 · September 2023

Approximation Algorithms for Quantum Max-ddd-Cut

Charlie Carlson, Zackary Jorquera, Alexandra Kolla, Steven Kordonowy, and Stuart Wayland

arXivpdf

Abstract.The Quantum Max-ddd-Cut problem seeks quantum states maximizing antisymmetric subspace energy in ddd-dimensional systems, generalizing Max-ddd-Cut. We provide a polynomial-time approximation algorithm with performance guarantees and prove its tightness using a gap instance for d≥3d \geq 3d≥3.
