Papers & preprints

Research

My work sits at the intersection of spectral graph theory, quantum Hamiltonian complexity, and randomized algorithms. I focus on classical algorithms for ground-state problems such as Quantum Max-Cut and the antiferromagnetic Heisenberg model, and on the spectral theory of random graphs and expanders.

    Classical Algorithms for Bipartite Quantum Max-Cut on Dense Expanders

    Stuart Wayland, Zackary Jorquera, and Alexandra Kolla

    arXiv:2610.03670, 2026. arXivpdf

    › Abstract

    We give a polynomial-time classical randomized algorithm for the spin-½ antiferromagnetic Heisenberg model — equivalently, Quantum Max-Cut — on dense, balanced bipartite expanders. The algorithm estimates the ground energy and edge correlations to additive error ε\varepsilonε in time polynomial in both the system size nnn and 1/ε1/\varepsilon1/ε.

  2. [2]
    Monogamy of Entanglement Bounds and Improved Approximation Algorithms for Qudit Hamiltonians

    Zackary Jorquera, Alexandra Kolla, Steven Kordonowy, Juspreet Singh Sandhu, and Stuart Wayland

    arXiv:2410.15544, 2024. arXivpdf

    › Abstract

    We establish new entanglement monogamy bounds for two-local qudit Hamiltonians and certify ground state energy using sum-of-squares proofs. A simple matching-based algorithm achieves better-than-random approximations, including a 0.595 guarantee for qubits, outperforming prior results on bounded-degree graphs.

  3. [3]
    Approximation Algorithms for Quantum Max-ddd-Cut

    Charlie Carlson, Zackary Jorquera, Alexandra Kolla, Steven Kordonowy, and Stuart Wayland

    arXiv:2309.10957, 2023. arXivpdf

    › Abstract

    The Quantum Max-ddd-Cut problem seeks quantum states maximizing antisymmetric subspace energy in ddd-dimensional systems, generalizing Max-ddd-Cut. We provide a polynomial-time approximation algorithm with performance guarantees and prove its tightness using a gap instance for d≥3d \geq 3d≥3.
