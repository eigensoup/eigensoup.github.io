---
title: "Quantifying Gerrymandering with Simulated Annealing"
date: 2022-06-14
summary: >-
  We apply Markov Chain Monte Carlo with Simulated Annealing to detect gerrymandering, showing
  it effectively generates representative voting distributions. Results on Texas demonstrate
  its speed and reliability in identifying bias in districting plans, offering a powerful
  analytical tool.
