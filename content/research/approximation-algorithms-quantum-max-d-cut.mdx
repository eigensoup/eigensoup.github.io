---
title: "Approximation Algorithms for Quantum Max-$d$-Cut"
date: 2023-09-19
authors: ["Charlie Carlson", "Zackary Jorquera", "Alexandra Kolla", "Steven Kordonowy", "Stuart Wayland"]
arxiv: "2309.10957"
summary: >-
  The Quantum Max-$d$-Cut problem seeks quantum states maximizing antisymmetric subspace energy
  in $d$-dimensional systems, generalizing Max-$d$-Cut. We provide a polynomial-time
  approximation algorithm with performance guarantees and prove its tightness using a gap
  instance for $d \geq 3$.
