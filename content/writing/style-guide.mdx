<MarginNote>The Heisenberg Hamiltonian, with one term per edge of $G$.</MarginNote>

$$
H_G \;=\; \sum_{(i,j) \in E} \frac{1}{4}\left( I - X_iX_j - Y_iY_j - Z_iZ_j \right).
$$

<Theorem kind="Lemma" n="1">
  If $G$ is $d$-regular, then the all-ones vector is an eigenvector of $A_G$ with eigenvalue $d$.
</Theorem>

<Proof>
  Each row of $A_G$ has exactly $d$ ones.
</Proof>
